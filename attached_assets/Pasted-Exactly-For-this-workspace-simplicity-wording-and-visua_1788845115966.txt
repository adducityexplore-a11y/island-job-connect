Exactly. For this workspace, **simplicity, wording, and visual hierarchy should be designed together**. The employer shouldn't feel like they've entered complicated HR software. It should feel like **The Jobs MV is taking hiring work off their hands**.

I would give Replit one focused redesign prompt covering the entire Employer Workspace, while protecting the functionality you already built.

One wording choice I'd particularly keep is **“Find Candidates For Me”** rather than “Start a hiring request.” The first sounds like the employer is **handing work to The Jobs MV**. The second sounds like they're starting another administrative process.

That small wording difference captures exactly what your platform is trying to achieve.
