Redesign and simplify the existing Hospitality Passport for The Jobs MV.

CORE IDEA:

The Hospitality Passport should be:

CV-FIRST
FAST TO COMPLETE
EASY TO UNDERSTAND
STRUCTURED FOR RECRUITMENT
MOBILE FRIENDLY

Candidates should NOT have to manually recreate everything already contained in their CV.

The ideal experience is:

UPLOAD CV
→ EXTRACT / PRE-FILL INFORMATION
→ CANDIDATE CONFIRMS IMPORTANT DETAILS
→ SAVE PASSPORT
→ READY FOR JOBS & TALENT SEARCH

Our goal is for a candidate to create a useful Hospitality Passport in approximately 3–5 minutes.

IMPORTANT:
- Improve the existing Hospitality Passport instead of creating a duplicate system.
- Preserve existing candidate accounts and saved data.
- Keep compatibility with recruiter Talent Search and candidate screening.
- Remove technical/database-style inputs from the candidate experience.
- Never show JSON fields to candidates.
- Never invent information from a CV.
- Extracted information must always be editable and confirmed by the candidate.
- Make the experience excellent on mobile.

--------------------------------------------------
19. PRIVACY
--------------------------------------------------

Candidates must control whether they are discoverable.

OPEN TO OPPORTUNITIES:

OFF

Candidate should not appear in general recruiter Talent Search.

ON

Candidate can be considered for relevant opportunities according to platform privacy rules.

Applying directly for a vacancy can still share the candidate's application information with that employer as required by the application process.

Do not expose:

Private phone number
Private email
Exact home address
Passport number
Government ID
Internal review notes

unless specifically required and permitted by the platform workflow.
