Update The Jobs MV homepage to communicate our company purpose more clearly and powerfully.

IMPORTANT:
- Keep the current visual design, colors, fonts, navigation and overall layout.
- Do not redesign the website.
- Keep it professional, clean and mobile-first.
- Avoid making the homepage too long.
- Do not use unsupported claims such as “#1”, “most trusted”, “leading” or “best”.
- Make the wording feel human, confident and hospitality-focused.

5. MOBILE EXPERIENCE

Make sure all new sections look excellent on mobile.

- Strong headline hierarchy
- Short readable paragraphs
- Comfortable spacing
- Full-width or easily tappable CTA buttons
- No overcrowded cards
- No horizontal overflow
- “People First” and “Time Matters” should stack vertically on small screens
- Employer message should remain highly visible on mobile


6. IMPORTANT BRAND POSITIONING

The Jobs MV should NOT look like another generic job board.

The homepage should clearly communicate that we solve two problems:

For Job Seekers:
Make it easier and faster to discover the right hospitality opportunities.

For Employers:
Reduce the time and effort required to find suitable hospitality candidates, especially for businesses that may not have dedicated recruitment resources.

The core idea throughout the website should be:

Right Talent. Right Opportunity. Right Time.

After implementation, test the homepage and About page on both desktop and mobile and make sure the existing Find Jobs, Hospitality Passport, employer and authentication functionality continues to work.
