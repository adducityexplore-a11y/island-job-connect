REDESIGN THE JOB SEEKER EXPERIENCE TO BE MOBILE-FIRST, SIMPLE, FAST AND GEN Z FRIENDLY

GOAL

Redesign and simplify The Jobs MV job seeker / candidate experience.

The majority of job seekers will use MOBILE PHONES.

Many users may come directly from:

- TikTok
- Instagram
- WhatsApp
- Telegram
- Shared job links
- Google search

The candidate experience must therefore feel:

- Fast
- Simple
- Mobile-native
- Modern
- Easy to understand
- Visually attractive
- Gen Z friendly
- Professional
- Hospitality-focused

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT make it look like complicated HR software.

Do NOT rebuild the candidate platform from scratch.

Audit the existing candidate pages, components, routes, authentication and data first.

Preserve all existing working functionality including:

- Candidate accounts
- Job listings
- Job details
- Hospitality Passport
- CV uploads
- Applications
- Saved jobs
- Candidate profile
- Open to Opportunities
- Existing database relationships
- Authentication

This task is about improving:

1. MOBILE UX
2. SIMPLICITY
3. WORDING
4. VISUAL HIERARCHY
5. JOB DISCOVERY
6. HOSPITALITY PASSPORT EXPERIENCE
7. APPLICATION EXPERIENCE
8. GEN Z CONNECTION


==================================================
CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE
==================================================

The candidate should NEVER feel:

"This is a long recruitment form."

They should feel:

"This is easy."

"I can find opportunities quickly."

"I can upload my CV once."

"I can build my profile without filling everything again."

"I can apply in a few taps."

"I can see what is happening with my application."


CORE EXPERIENCE:

FIND OPPORTUNITIES
↓
BUILD YOUR HOSPITALITY PASSPORT
↓
APPLY EASILY
↓
TRACK YOUR APPLICATIONS
↓
STAY READY FOR THE NEXT OPPORTUNITY


==================================================
IMPORTANT GEN Z PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Do NOT try to make the platform Gen Z friendly by making it childish.

Do NOT add:

- Neon colors
- Excessive emojis
- Cartoon graphics
- Too many animations
- Gaming-style UI
- Slang everywhere
- Social-media clutter

GEN Z FRIENDLY should mean:

- Fast
- Visual
- Short wording
- Easy navigation
- Mobile-first
- Clear actions
- Fewer fields
- Fewer clicks
- Good spacing
- Modern cards
- Strong imagery
- Smooth interactions


Use this principle:

LESS READING.
FEWER FIELDS.
FEWER CLICKS.
MORE OPPORTUNITIES.


==================================================
CANDIDATE BRAND POSITIONING
==================================================

Candidate side should communicate:

MORE THAN A JOB SEARCH


Headline:

Your experience deserves to be seen.


Supporting copy:

Build your Hospitality Passport, discover hospitality opportunities and apply without filling out the same information again and again.

==================================================
MOBILE-FIRST HOMEPAGE / CANDIDATE HOME
==================================================

The mobile experience is the highest priority.

Do NOT make the candidate home look like the Employer dashboard.

Avoid four corporate KPI cards at the top.

Instead create a personal, simple home experience.


Example:

Hi Ahmed 👋

Ready for your next opportunity?


Then show one important action based on the user's situation.


IF NEW USER:

Build your Hospitality Passport

Upload your CV and create your hospitality profile in a few minutes.

[ Start My Passport ]


IF PASSPORT INCOMPLETE:

Your Passport is 70% ready

Add your availability to improve your profile.

[ Continue Passport ]


IF COMPLETE:

Your Passport is ready

Open to Opportunities: ON

[ View Passport ]


Use real candidate name and data only.


==================================================
LATEST OPPORTUNITIES
==================================================

Show job discovery very high on the page.

Heading:

Latest Opportunities

or:

Opportunities For You


Do not bury jobs below many informational sections.


Job cards should be easy to scan in 2-3 seconds.


Example:

BARTENDER

ABC Maldives Resort

Noonu Atoll

USD 800–1,000 / month

Service Charge

Accommodation

Posted 2 days ago


[ View Opportunity ]

Save icon


Do not show too much description inside cards.

- Location

Too many tags

Too many buttons

Technical recruitment information

==================================================

UPLOAD CV
↓

Continue


Secondary:

Save & Exit


Keep each step short.

APPLICATION EXPERIENCE
==================================================

Applying should be extremely fast.

Primary CTA on job page:

If logged out:

Sign in / Create Account

If Passport has enough information:

Show a short review screen.

Example:

You're applying for:

Bartender

ABC Maldives Resort


Using:

Your Hospitality Passport

Your CV


[ Submit Application ]

==================================================
FINAL DESIGN TEST
==================================================

Ask:

Can a young candidate who has never used a recruitment website understand the platform without instructions?

Can they find a job within seconds?

Can they build a Passport without getting bored?

Can they apply without retyping their CV?

Can they understand their application status?

Can they use everything comfortably with one hand on a phone?


If not:

SIMPLIFY FURTHER.
