6. CONFIDENCE LEVELS
==================================================

For extracted information support:

CONFIRMED
LIKELY
UNCERTAIN
NOT_FOUND
NEEDS_CONFIRMATION


Recommended logic:

CONFIRMED:
Directly supported by clear evidence.

LIKELY:
Strong contextual support but not explicit.

UNCERTAIN:
Possible but ambiguous.

NOT_FOUND:
No supporting information.

NEEDS_CONFIRMATION:
Important information not reliably available.


Avoid showing uncertain information to employers as fact.
