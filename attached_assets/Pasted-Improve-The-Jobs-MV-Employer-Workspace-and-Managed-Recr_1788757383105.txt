Improve The Jobs MV Employer Workspace and Managed Recruitment Request experience.

IMPORTANT:
- Work with the EXISTING Employer Workspace.
- Do not rebuild the application from scratch.
- Preserve existing authentication, database relationships, vacancies, applications and employer accounts.
- Keep the current general brand identity, but improve UI/UX where necessary.
- Do not create duplicate recruitment systems.
- Make the workflow extremely simple for employers.
- Mobile experience is a priority.
- Do not use fake statistics or fake candidates.
- All counts and statuses must come from real data.

CORE BUSINESS IDEA:

The Jobs MV offers employers TWO ways to recruit:

OPTION 1:
POST A VACANCY

Employer manages recruitment themselves.

Post Vacancy
→ Receive Applications
→ Review
→ Shortlist
→ Interview
→ Hire


OPTION 2:
REQUEST CANDIDATES

The Jobs MV helps manage the recruitment process.

Tell Us Who You Need
→ We Source
→ We Screen
→ Experts Review
→ Interview-Ready Shortlist
→ Employer Interviews
→ Hire


The second option is one of the main differentiators of The Jobs MV.

The experience should communicate:

"Tell us who you need. We'll help find the right people."
