Upgrade the AI candidate screening system for The Jobs MV.

MAIN OBJECTIVE:

Create a realistic, explainable AI recruitment screening system specifically designed for Maldives hospitality recruitment.

Do NOT allow AI to make final hiring decisions.

The AI should assist HR by comparing candidates against clearly defined vacancy requirements.

---

1. THREE TYPES OF JOB REQUIREMENTS

For every vacancy create:

MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS

PREFERRED REQUIREMENTS

OPERATIONAL REQUIREMENTS

Example for Bartender:

Mandatory:

* Minimum 2 years relevant experience
* Bartending experience
* English communication

Preferred:

* Maldives resort experience
* Luxury resort experience
* Cocktail knowledge
* Wine knowledge
* POS experience
* WSET certification

Operational:

* Local / international recruitment eligibility
* Required joining date
* Notice period
* Number of vacancies
* Salary range
* Work permit sponsorship availability where relevant

Allow recruiter/admin to edit these requirements.

---

2. CUSTOM WEIGHTING

Do NOT use one fixed AI scoring formula for every vacancy.

Allow recruiter to define importance.

Example:

Relevant Experience: 25%
Position Experience: 20%
Resort Experience: 20%
Technical Skills: 15%
Qualifications: 10%
Availability: 10%

Total: 100%

Allow requirements to be marked:

Required
Preferred
Not Important

---

3. EXPLAINABLE AI MATCH SCORE

Generate an overall candidate match score.

Example:

Overall Match: 86%

Relevant Experience: 25/25
Position Experience: 20/20
Maldives / Resort Experience: 15/20
Technical Skills: 13/15
Qualifications: 5/10
Availability: 8/10

Never display an AI score without explanation.

---

4. AI ASSESSMENT

For each candidate generate:

Strong Matches

Potential Concerns

Missing Requirements

Information Not Found

Needs HR Verification

Example:

STRONG MATCHES

* 5 years bartending experience
* 3 years Maldives resort experience
* Strong cocktail experience
* Micros POS experience

MISSING / NOT FOUND

* No WSET certification found

NEEDS HR VERIFICATION

* Notice period unclear
* Work permit status not provided

Do NOT automatically rank candidates higher or lower based purely on nationality.

Create explicit recruitment settings:

International Candidates Accepted

Current country

Work authorization status

Joining availability

Notice period

Do not claim legal employment eligibility without verification.

"Employer verification required."

---

10. EVIDENCE-BASED AI SCREENING

Every important AI finding should reference the information found in the candidate CV/profile.

Example:

Maldives Experience: STRONG

Evidence:
"Worked as Bartender at XYZ Maldives Resort from March 2022 to January 2025."

Do not make assumptions when evidence is unavailable.

---

11. CONFIDENCE

For extracted information show:

Confirmed from CV

Candidate Provided

AI Inferred

Needs Verification

Avoid presenting AI inference as fact.

---

12. AI INTERVIEW QUESTIONS

After screening create:

"Questions to Verify"

Generate questions based on missing or unclear information.

Example:

* Are you currently working in Maldives?
* What is your notice period?
* Do you currently have an active Maldives work permit?
* Which POS systems have you used?
* Describe your cocktail preparation experience.
* How many guests/covers did your previous outlet normally handle?

---

13. HUMAN DECISION

Always display:

"AI recommendations are provided to assist recruiters. Final screening and hiring decisions remain with the employer."

Recruiter must be able to:

Override AI score

Add HR notes

Manually shortlist

Manually reject

Move candidate to interview

Mark selected/hired

---

14. PROTECTED CHARACTERISTICS

Never use the following to automatically increase or decrease candidate match score:

Gender
Religion
Race
Ethnicity
Photo
Disability
Age unless an explicit lawful job requirement has been configured and legally validated

Nationality must not silently influence the candidate score.

Recruitment restrictions must be explicitly configured at vacancy level.

---

15. ADMIN KNOWLEDGE BASE

Create an admin-controlled section called:

Maldives Recruitment Knowledge Base

Admin can maintain:

Hospitality job titles
Equivalent job titles
Career hierarchy
Common resort skills
Certifications
Maldives recruitment terminology
Work permit guidance
Recruitment policy guidance
Screening rules

AI screening should use this knowledge base.

Do NOT hard-code policies permanently into prompts.

Allow administrators to update them as regulations and recruitment practices change.

---

16. POLICY VERSIONING

Add:

Policy Name
Source
Effective Date
Last Reviewed
Status
Notes

Example:

Maldives Work Visa Process
Source: Maldives Immigration
Last Reviewed: September 2026

This allows The Jobs MV to keep recruitment guidance updated when Maldives regulations change.

---

17. MAIN PRINCIPLE

The AI should answer:

"How well does this candidate match THIS vacancy?"

It should NOT answer:

"Should this person be hired?"

Final hiring decisions must always remain with the recruiter.
